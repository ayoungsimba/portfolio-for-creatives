Cura - Intelligent Photos App

A completely new product design that reimagines the role photos play in our lives. More than a place to store photos, a true memory companion.


Role

Product Designer (UI/UX)

Team

Independent project

Year

2025—2026

Tools

Figma

Photoshop

After Effects

Problem

Most users are overwhelmed by their photo libraries (filled with tens of thousands of images) but rarely revisit or organize them.

Opportunity

Design a photo app that acts as a creative, intelligent memory companion, helping users revisit, reflect, and relive their most meaningful moments.

How Might We?

Transform digital clutter into meaningful, creative, and context-rich memories?

Key Insight

Photos without any meaning become digital clutter. We're going to create an app that helps you sort through the chaos & rediscover the moments that matter.

Final Takeaway

The photos we keep deserve more than storage. We aimed to make digital memories feel human again.

A calmer, more contextual photo system: one that helps people rediscover images as living memories rather than an endless count:
